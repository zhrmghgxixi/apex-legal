2.What the app does

The App is the companion application for our electronic heating device (model MS‑6300‑A, product name APEX). It is a remote control, not a content or social service. After connecting to the device over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) you can:

  • scan for and connect to nearby APEX devices;
  • read device information (product model, serial number, hardware and firmware version, battery level);
  • choose a working mode and set the target temperature and duration of each stage;
  • start, pause, resume, skip a preheat stage, and stop heating;
  • view diagnostic data and run calibration;
  • install a firmware update (OTA) from a .bin file stored on your phone.

4.How Bluetooth data flows

All communication happens directly between your phone and the device over BLE, a short‑range radio link. There is no relay server, no cloud account and no vendor back end. Range is a few metres; the link is encrypted at the BLE link layer where the device supports it.
